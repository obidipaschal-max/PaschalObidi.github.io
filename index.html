Who I Am

The philosopher
who ships.

I hold two First Class degrees in Philosophy — from Nnamdi Azikiwe University, Awka and Urbaniana University, Rome — and I am an IBM Certified Product Manager. That combination is not accidental. It is the foundation of everything I build.

Most AI product leaders ask "what can this do?" I also ask "what should it do, and for whom?" That second question — the ethical one, the philosophical one — is increasingly the most important question in product. And it's the one most teams skip.

I have built communities from zero, authored original frameworks, shipped PRDs, hosted live AI philosophy spaces, and founded Nigeria's dedicated Cardano ecosystem. I operate at the intersection of AI strategy, product execution, and ethical governance.

Intellectual Property

Original frameworks.

These are not borrowed methodologies. They are frameworks I built, named, and published — each emerging from a real product challenge or philosophical inquiry. All IP attributed to Paschal Obidi.

🏚️
Ghost Mall Paradox™
Economic Theory · AI & Labor Markets
An original economic theory arguing that AI-driven job displacement is ultimately self-correcting — because capitalism's circular flow mechanics require consumers with purchasing power. When AI destroys jobs, it destroys its own market. The paradox forces a reckoning: augmentation over replacement is not just ethical, it is economically inevitable.
"AI cannot automate away the customer."
👑
Human Premium™
Value Framework · Post-AI Economy
A framework defining the irreducible human value proposition in an AI-saturated economy. Identifies three premium signals — emotional intelligence, ethical judgment, and contextual creativity — that AI cannot replicate at commercial scale. Designed for individuals navigating career strategy and for organizations building human-AI hybrid teams.
"In an age of infinite AI output, irreplaceable humans become the scarcest resource."
⚙️
PACE Framework
PM Methodology · AI Agent Integration
A product management methodology for integrating AI agents into PM workflows: Plan (define scope and constraints), Assign (delegate to the right AI agent or human), Connect (link outputs across tools and teams), Evaluate (measure and iterate). Built for AI-era PMs who need structure without rigidity.
"Speed matters. But what matters most is real impact on users."
📡
VTV Strategy
GTM Framework · Applied at Farmroll
A go-to-market framework built for zero-budget ecosystem building: Visibility (get discovered before you're understood), Trend (attach your product to a wave already moving), Virality (design for the second share, not the first). Designed and executed at Farmroll. Now a reusable commercial framework.
"Build it and they'll come is a prayer, not a strategy."

The Destination

Building toward CAIO.

I am not building toward a Chief AI Officer role because it is a prestigious title. I am building toward it because it is the only role that requires everything I am: philosophical depth, product execution, ethical judgment, and strategic vision.

Most AI leaders have one or two of these. Very few have all four. The organizations that will win the next decade of AI won't be the ones who deploy the most models — they will be the ones who deploy them wisely, ethically, and with a clear answer to the question: why?

The CAIO role is not yet standardized. Most organizations don't know what they need from it yet. That ambiguity is not a problem for me — it is an opportunity to define the role from first principles. Which is exactly how I approach every product challenge I encounter.

"I don't just ask what AI can do. I ask what it should do, and why. That second question is increasingly the most important one in product."

— Paschal Obidi
